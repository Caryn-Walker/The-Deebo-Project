Fitness & Personal Training

Ex-British Army and fully qualified personal trainer, dedicated to helping individuals transform both physically and mentally.

I specialise in highly demanding training programmes, strength and conditioning, weight loss, muscle building and endurance, tailoring every programme to each individual's goals and abilities.

Fitness is about more than simply training in the gym. I provide personalised nutrition advice, 24/7 support and guidance, and practical life coaching designed to build confidence, discipline and a winning mindset.

My approach combines military discipline with realistic, sustainable methods that deliver long-term results.

Through one-to-one coaching, motivation and accountability, I help clients develop the habits, mindset and resilience needed to succeed not only in fitness, but in everyday life.

Fitness Holidays & Retreats

For those looking for a complete change of environment, I also offer fitness holidays and retreats abroad in the sun.

These retreats combine intensive training, nutrition guidance, relaxation and personal development, as well as having a Life Coach on hand to offer guidance, eliminate obstacles and help you stay on track.

Services Include

  • Personal training and strength coaching
  • Weight loss and body transformation
  • Muscle building and conditioning
  • Nutrition advice and meal guidance
  • 24/7 support and accountability
  • Life coaching and mindset development
  • Confidence building and motivation
  • Overseas fitness holidays and retreats
  • Training available throughout the UK and Europe

Deebo Bespoke Carpentry

Every piece tells a story; yours starts here.

From custom shelving and tables to unique, one-off pieces, every project is built with precision and attention to detail.

I specialise in creating handcrafted bespoke furniture and carpentry, built completely from scratch using reclaimed timber chosen by you.

Every grain, every finish and every detail is carefully crafted to create something that is truly one of a kind.

I'm fully mobile, bringing the workshop to you.

Whether it's a custom piece for your home or building it on-site as part of your space, you're involved throughout the journey, making every project as personal as the finished result.

Over the years I've travelled throughout Europe, creating bespoke furniture for clients who wanted something unique, timeless and built to last.
